How It Works

Every hour, CassadyNet pulls stories from over 100 RSS feeds spanning tech news, AI lab blogs, research papers, international sources, and community discussions. An AI system then scores each story based on newsworthiness, impact, and relevance. Only the top stories make it to the homepage.

What Makes Us Different

  • Speed First — We prioritize breaking news over evergreen content
  • Global Coverage — Sources from Asia, Europe, and beyond, not just Silicon Valley
  • Signal Over Noise — AI filters out PR fluff, product reviews, and off-topic stories
  • No Paywalls — Free access, no account required
  • No Tracking — We don't collect your data or use cookies
