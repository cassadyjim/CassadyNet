Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 13, 2026

The short version: CassadyNet does not collect, store, or share any personal information about our visitors.

Who We Are

CassadyNet is an AI news aggregation website. We curate and link to AI-related news stories from third-party sources.

Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information from visitors to our website. Specifically:

  • We do not use cookies or tracking technologies
  • We do not collect names, email addresses, or contact information
  • We do not use any service that tracks or profiles individual visitors
  • We do not require account registration

Analytics

We use GoatCounter, a privacy-focused analytics tool, to see aggregate visit counts (such as total pageviews and which pages are popular). GoatCounter does not use cookies, does not use tracking scripts that follow you across sites, and does not build profiles of individual visitors. It is not possible for us to identify or track any specific visitor through this data. See GoatCounter's privacy policy for details.

Third-Party Links

CassadyNet contains links to external websites and news sources. When you click on a link to leave our site, you will be directed to a third-party website. We have no control over, and assume no responsibility for, the content, privacy policies, or practices of any third-party websites.

We encourage you to review the privacy policy of every site you visit.

Web Hosting

Our website is hosted by third-party hosting providers who may collect standard server logs (such as IP addresses and access times) as part of their normal operations. We do not access or use this data for any purpose.

Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed at children under the age of 13. Since we do not collect any personal information, we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Your continued use of the website after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
