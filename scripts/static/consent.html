📱 SMS Text Message Consent

By providing your phone number and opting in to receive SMS text messages from CassadyNet, you expressly consent to the following:

"I agree to receive SMS text messages from CassadyNet at the phone number I have provided. I understand that message and data rates may apply. I can opt out at any time by replying STOP."

This consent is separate from any other agreements, including our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

What You Are Agreeing To Receive

By opting in, you agree to receive the following types of SMS text messages from CassadyNet:

Types of SMS Text Messages You Will Receive:

  • System Notifications – Alerts about account activity or system status
  • Reminder Messages – Time-sensitive reminders and updates
  • Workflow Alerts – Automated notifications based on your preferences
  • Service Updates – Important information about CassadyNet services

Message Frequency: Message frequency varies. You may receive up to 10 SMS text messages per month depending on your notification preferences and account activity.

How to Opt-In to SMS Text Messages

You may opt-in to receive SMS text messages from CassadyNet through the following methods:

  • Web Form: By entering your phone number and checking the SMS consent checkbox on our website
  • Keyword Opt-In: By texting JOIN to our designated phone number
  • Account Registration: By providing your phone number during account setup and agreeing to receive SMS messages

In each case, you will receive a confirmation SMS text message verifying your opt-in.

How to Opt-Out of SMS Text Messages

You may opt out of receiving SMS text messages at any time by replying:

STOP

After opting out, you will receive a final confirmation message and will no longer receive SMS text messages from CassadyNet.

How to Get Help

For assistance with SMS text messages, reply:

HELP

You will receive instructions and contact information for further support.

Message and Data Rates

Standard message and data rates may apply. Please contact your mobile carrier for details about your text messaging plan. CassadyNet is not responsible for any charges from your mobile carrier.

Supported Carriers

SMS text messages are supported on all major US carriers including AT&T, Verizon, T-Mobile, Sprint, and others. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy and Data Protection

Your phone number and SMS messaging data:

  • Will only be used to send you the SMS text messages you have consented to receive
  • Will NOT be sold, rented, or shared with third parties for marketing purposes
  • Will be stored securely in accordance with our Privacy Policy

No Purchase Required

Consent to receive SMS text messages is not a condition of any purchase. You may use CassadyNet services without opting in to SMS messaging.
